## Registration

For any course, please send the full amount with your name, address and telephone number, stating the course you wish to attend and the date and time it starts. The Centre will accept no liability for lost or misdirected bookings and deposit cheques, unless due to its own negligence.

## Online Registration

We recommend that you use our online registration and payment options.

## Telephone Bookings

Telephone bookings by credit card (Visa or Mastercard) or debit card are accepted.

## Refund or Credit

Please note, no refunds will be made for any reason. Credit can be given for deposits or course payments only, if notified at least 24 hours in advance. There is a fee for transferring payment from one course to another, if informed 48 hours before the start of the course. We accept Visa, Mastercard, debit, cheque and cash.

Cancellation of a course by the Centre will result in a full refund of any monies paid.

## Registered Charity

The Sivananda Yoga Vedanta Centre is a registered charity. We try to keep prices to a minimum. Donations of all kinds are appreciated. All donations other than class donations are tax deductible.
