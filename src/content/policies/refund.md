Please note, no refunds will be made for any reason.

Credit can be given for deposits or course payments only, if notified at least 24 hours in advance.

There is a $20 fee for transferring payment from one course to another, if informed 48 hours before the start of the course.

We accept Visa, Mastercard, debit, cheque and cash.

The Sivananda Yoga Vedanta Centre is a registered charity. We try to keep prices to a minimum. Donations of all kinds are appreciated. All donations other than class donations are tax deductible.
