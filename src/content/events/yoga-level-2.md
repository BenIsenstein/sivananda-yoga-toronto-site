This course builds on the foundation laid in our Yoga Level 1 course and gives you the opportunity to build up a solid practice, including all the basic postures and breathing exercises.

Emphasis is placed on holding postures longer and making smooth transitions between postures. You will also discover some variations of the basic yoga postures, deepen the practice of breathing techniques, and practise Headstand.

This is an ideal refresher class for those who have lapsed in their practice. It is also suitable for experienced practitioners who are new to the Sivananda Yoga Center.
