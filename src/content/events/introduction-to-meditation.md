Meditation is an invaluable tool for emotional balance and peace of mind. This course provides step-by-step guidance for starting a meditation practice. It is helpful for those who are just starting, as well as those who would like to renew their commitment to this practice.

Topics covered include:

- What is meditation and what are the benefits?
- How to start a meditation practice: the three fundamentals.
- Meditation for beginners, and tips for returning practitioners.
