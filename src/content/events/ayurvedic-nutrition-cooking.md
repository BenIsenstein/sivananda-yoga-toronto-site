A 10-hour certificate of completion is awarded after successful attendance. Schedule: 1–2:30 pm theory; 3–6 pm cooking session (followed by a meal!).

This course is designed to provide students with a basic understanding of Ayurvedic nutrition and food combinations that promote health and digestion. Students will learn how to plan well-balanced, nutritious vegetarian meals made from grains, beans, vegetables, fruits, seeds, dairy products, fats, spices and herbs, based on Ayurvedic principles.

Ayurveda — a traditional, holistic, natural healing science of health from India — has been practised for over 5,000 years and teaches us how to live a healthy, balanced life. Ayurveda emphasizes diet as the most essential part of a healthy life. The course is open to everyone.

## Course overview (theory)

- A balanced diet based on Ayurvedic principles
- The twelve Ayurvedic food groups
- Sources and balance of the six tastes (shad rasa)
- Food combinations using Ayurvedic principles
- Vegetarian proteins for maintaining essential protein in the diet
- Ayurvedic herbs and spices to enhance and regulate digestion
- The concepts of Agni and Ama
- The concept of fasting (Upvasa)
- Planning well-balanced, nutritious meals

## Cooking workshops overview

- Tri-Doshic recipes for all body types
- Recipes cooked using healing herbs and spices
- Proper food combinations to enhance digestion
- Preparation of nutritious sweets and healthy hot and cold drinks

**Teacher:** Dr. Sonal Bhatt, M.D. (Ayurveda) is a highly respected Ayurvedic physician and Panchakarma specialist from Jamnagar Ayurvedic University, one of the leading Ayurvedic universities in India. She is also a Registered Holistic Nutritional Consultant from CSNN Toronto and has practised as an Ayurvedic consultant, Panchakarma specialist, teacher and researcher in India, Canada and North America for over 30 years.
