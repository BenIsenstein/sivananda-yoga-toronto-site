Step into a warm, welcoming space and discover the heart-opening practice of Kirtan with Shell and Anton, who are on their Canadian Kirtan Tour and are coming to our yoga centre as one of their stops to share in this Bhakti Yoga practice.

Kirtan is a simple, joyful form of group meditation that blends live music with call-and-response mantra chanting. You don't need any singing experience — just a willingness to listen, repeat, and be part of the shared sound. There's no pressure to perform. We repeat the mantras to express, not to impress.

With the musical guidance of harmonium and percussion, Shell and Anton will lead us through each mantra at a relaxed pace. They will share short stories and clear explanations so you can understand the meaning and intention behind what you're chanting. You are always welcome to join in, listen quietly, dance, or simply soak in the atmosphere.

Whether you are completely new to Kirtan or returning to a beloved practice, this is a space to unwind, connect, and experience the calming, uplifting power of chanting together. Come as you are — your voice and your presence are enough.

**Facilitators:** Shell and Anton are yoga educators and international touring Kirtan artists who walk the joyful path of Bhakti Yoga. With over 35 years of combined practice, they have found their deepest inspiration in Kirtan — musical mantra chanting that both soothes the mind and lifts the spirit. They have deepened their studies on several spiritual journeys through India guided by their beloved guru and teacher, Sri Prasad Rangnekar, based in Mumbai.
