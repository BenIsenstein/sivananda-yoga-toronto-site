Escape the noise of everyday life and immerse yourself in this Ayurveda and Yoga retreat at the Sivananda Yoga Center. Yoga and Ayurveda are complementary Vedic disciplines with many therapeutic benefits for the health of body and mind. This urban retreat begins with meditation, followed by a yoga class and a workshop on Ayurvedic nutrition and cooking.

## Schedule

- 10 am – 12 noon: Meditation and yoga class
- 12 noon – 1 pm: Tea and snacks; yoga and Ayurveda discussion
- 1 – 4 pm: Ayurvedic cooking workshop
- 4 – 4:30 pm: Ayurvedic lunch

**Teachers:** Dr. Sonal Bhatt, M.D. (Ayurveda) is a highly respected Ayurvedic physician and Panchakarma specialist from Jamnagar Ayurvedic University, one of the leading Ayurvedic universities in India. She is also a Registered Holistic Nutritional Consultant from CSNN Toronto, with over 30 years of practice in India, Canada and North America. She is joined by the Sivananda Yoga Center teachers.
