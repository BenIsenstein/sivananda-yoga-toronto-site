This 3-hour workshop introduces a simple approach to yoga corrections:

**Observe → Analyze → Adjust**

We will apply this approach to the 12 basic asanas of the Sivananda sequence and Sun Salutations, learning how to identify what needs correction and use clear verbal cues and hands-on adjustments to guide students safely and effectively.

Most of the workshop will be hands-on. You will work with partners to practise giving and receiving adjustments, with guidance and feedback throughout.

Designed for yoga teachers who wish to deepen their skills in guiding students to practise safely and correctly, while helping them gain the maximum benefits from their practice.

**Teacher:** Anantya has been teaching yoga classes and workshops at the Sivananda Yoga Centre and in the Toronto community for many years. He is an experienced and dedicated teacher who guides students with clarity, care, and attention to individual needs. Anantya is an E-RYT 500 and a Yoga Alliance Continuing Education Provider (YACEP).
