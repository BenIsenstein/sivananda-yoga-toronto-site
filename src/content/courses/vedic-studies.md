Our Vedic Studies offerings, taught by **Ram Vakkalanka**, explore Sanskrit, Vedic astrology and the Vedic sciences.

## Sacred Vibrations of Sanskrit — Sanskrit for Beginners

Sanskrit is an eternal, vibrant language of most ancient origins — a collection of the subtle vibrations of the Cosmic Energies, and called the 'Language of the Gods.' Chanting and contemplating upon Sanskrit sounds is very energizing and helps in attuning oneself to Cosmic Consciousness. Sanskrit is Yoga in language-form: its practice opens channels for the flow of subtle, vibrational energies through the mind, just as the practice of Yoga opens channels for the flow of energy through the body.

'The Sacred Vibrations of Sanskrit' covers four aspects of this wonderful language:

1. Basic spoken Sanskrit.
2. Accurate and authentic pronunciation of some energizing Mantras and chants.
3. Meaning and authentic pronunciation of the Sanskrit names of popular yoga poses.
4. Introduction to the Sanskrit alphabet and its experiential basis in the body of the practitioner.

## Vedic Astrology for Beginners

Jyotisha, or Vedic Astrology, is a powerful tool that can help you understand yourself deeply and profoundly. It throws light on the path of your life, helps you understand the karmic tendencies buried within your psyche, and can help you liberate yourself from the past and unleash your fullest potential. Teachers and practitioners of Yoga, Feng Shui, Reiki and Ayurveda often find Vedic Astrology useful in their professions.

## Your teacher

**Ram Vakkalanka** was born and brought up in India, in a family dedicated to a yogic style of living. He grew up studying the Sanskrit language, the Bhagavad Gita and the Upanishads, and chanting Mantras as a lifestyle. In his teens he began studying music through the Sitar, eventually leading to Nada Yoga. In 2010 he left a career in corporate management to dedicate his life to spreading the philosophy of the Veda and Vedic sciences such as Astrology and Ayurveda. He shares his deep knowledge with a global audience; his teachings are simple but profound, filled with current examples and humour. Learn more at [ramvakkalanka.com](http://www.ramvakkalanka.com/).
