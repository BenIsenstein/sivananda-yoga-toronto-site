Pranayama (breathing exercises) helps strengthen the respiratory system, increase stamina and maintain good physical health. It also helps keep the mind calm and peaceful. We periodically offer focused pranayama courses and challenges — including guided morning practices combining Sun Salutations to warm up the body, extended pranayama, and guided relaxation.

Some practices assume familiarity with **Kapalabhati** and **Anuloma Viloma**; suitability is noted for each offering.

## Some of the benefits of pranayama

- Optimal gas exchange (O₂ and CO₂).
- Oxygenation of tissues thanks to breath retention.
- Harmonization of metabolic rates and the chemical balance of the body.
- Clearness of mind and stimulation of the parasympathetic system.
- Improvement of digestion and fortification of the immune system.
