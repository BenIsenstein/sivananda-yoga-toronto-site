Alongside our Hatha Yoga practice, we offer courses and talks exploring the philosophy of **Vedanta** and the yogic path. These teachings help students understand the mind, the four paths of yoga (Karma, Bhakti, Raja and Jnana), and how yogic philosophy applied in daily life supports a peaceful mind.

Philosophy offerings vary through the year and include kirtan (devotional chanting), satsang, and guest-led courses. To learn more about the tradition we teach, see [What We Teach](/about/what-we-teach).

Check our [workshops and events](/events) for current philosophy courses and talks.
